A LITTLE MORE ABOUT ME

Curiosity, with a
practical side.

I’m a final-year Computer Networks and Communications student at UIT, VNU-HCM, working toward a career as a Research Engineer. I explore how networks can be understood, optimized, and operated through digital twins, learning, and careful experimentation.

My Vietnamese name is Đoàn Văn Tài. I’m based in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam, and expect to complete my Bachelor of Engineering in Computer Networks and Communications in 2027.

What I’m working toward

I want to develop as a Research Engineer: someone who can engage with a research question, turn it into a working implementation, and evaluate the result carefully. In the longer term, I’m interested in pursuing graduate research in networks and intelligent systems.

What draws my attention

Network Digital Twins, network optimization, and AI / ML for networking are the core of my current work. I’m particularly interested in what happens when the information available to a decision-maker is stale, incomplete, or imperfectly modeled.

That interest connects the systems side of my work—SDN testbeds, synchronization, and cloud infrastructure—with questions about uncertainty, routing, and decision quality.

How I approach a project

  • Start with a concrete question. Decide what the system or experiment should help us understand.
  • Build something inspectable. Keep the implementation, assumptions, and evaluation connected.
  • Keep the limits visible. A working pipeline is a useful starting point; it is not, by itself, evidence that a hypothesis is true.
