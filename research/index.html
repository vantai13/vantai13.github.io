RESEARCH

Research

Two connected studies: building a Network Digital Twin and examining the routing decisions a controller makes with imperfect information.

What is a Network Digital Twin?

A Network Digital Twin is a digital representation of a network that uses operational data to reflect its state and support analysis, prediction, or control.

Current research question

For load-changing routing actions, how do improvements in current-state information and action-response model fidelity differ and interact in their effect on expected decision regret?

From DT4N to State–Model–Regret

In DT4N, I implemented the observation and control paths of an SDN testbed. The case study explains the architecture, how control actions are checked, and the limits of the experiments.

State–Model–Regret uses controlled two-path queueing models to separate the effects of current-state information and response modeling. It remains exploratory research; the case study records the comparison scope, evidence, and unresolved questions.
